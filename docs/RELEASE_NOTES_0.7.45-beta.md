### Deadlock Dolly v0.7.45-beta

**0.7.45-beta**

- **Much faster camera exports.** *Before:* exporting a long camera move to After Effects or USD could take several seconds, and grew slower with every keyframe. *After:* the same export finishes almost instantly, with exactly the same camera data.

- **Shot names keep their letters in USD files.** *Before:* a shot named in Cyrillic, Japanese or with emoji showed up as question marks in the exported USD camera. *After:* the name is saved exactly as you typed it.
