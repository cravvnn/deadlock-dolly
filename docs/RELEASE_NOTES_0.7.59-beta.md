### Deadlock Dolly v0.7.59-beta

**0.7.59-beta**

- **Older Dolly versions read your preferences again.** *Before:* after using 0.7.58, opening an older copy of Dolly showed "Unsupported Dolly settings version: 12" and started with default shortcuts. *After:* Dolly saves your preferences in a format every version can read.

- **Your 0.7.58 preferences carry over.** *Before:* 0.7.58 kept the replay list's sort inside your preferences file. *After:* the first time 0.7.59 opens, your shortcuts, folders and other settings are kept as they are, and the replay list still remembers its sort.
