### Deadlock Dolly v0.7.66-beta

**0.7.66-beta**

- **Restored the cleaner 0.7.63-beta falling rain and removed ground splash rings.** *Before:* 0.7.64-beta made falling rain overly dense and bright, and stamped repeating circular splash droplets across wet surfaces. *After:* falling rain returns to the natural, unobtrusive 0.7.63-beta look and ground splash rings are removed, while keeping wet-ground sheen, puddles and puddle ripples.

- **Video exports respect the shot's Standard or High weather quality.** *Before:* fixed-step video exports always forced High weather quality even when the shot was set to Standard. *After:* exports follow the shot's chosen Standard or High weather quality while keeping full-resolution raymarching.
