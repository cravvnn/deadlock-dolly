### Deadlock Dolly v0.7.57-beta

**0.7.57-beta**

- **Snow, sand and ash settle on the map.** *Before:* the Scene tab's snow, sand and ash covered the ground as a thin, even layer. *After:* the cover is thick and shaped by the map: it banks up smoothly against walls and into corners, fills steps, builds into drifts over a shot and creeps in under shelter from the upwind side. Rain storms leave puddles with reflections.

- **Heroes leave tracks.** *Before:* heroes walked over snow, sand and ash without marking it. *After:* turn on **Hero tracks** under **Scene → Coating**: heroes cut tracks into the cover that fill back in over time. The tracks fade out when a hero jumps and pick up again where they land. There is no record step: Dolly reads the hero paths from the replay in the background when you open it, so tracks show anywhere in the replay, even on parts you have not played.

- **Kick-up behind running heroes.** *Before:* nothing rose from the ground when heroes ran through snow or sand. *After:* a soft, low cloud trails behind running heroes, including from a camera following them.

- **Snow lands on heroes and troopers.** *Before:* characters stood on top of the cover. *After:* they stand in it and catch the falling snow, sand or ash, with no pale ring around them.

- **Finer falling snow, sand and ash.** *Before:* falling particles were similar-sized streaks. *After:* most are fine specks, with fewer big flakes or grains, closer to real snowfall and blowing sand.

- **Smoother weather.** *Before:* the first Play with weather could stall while shaders compiled, and close-ups were costly. *After:* shaders compile in the background, and close-ups cost about a third less at 1440p and above. Blizzard is still demanding, so expect a lower frame rate on slower PCs; frame-accurate exports render at full quality.
