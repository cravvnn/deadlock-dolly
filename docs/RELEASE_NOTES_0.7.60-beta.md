### Deadlock Dolly v0.7.60-beta

**0.7.60-beta**

- **Your replay speed stays where you set it.** *Before:* after playing a camera path, seeking or finishing a recording, the replay went back to 1x and you had to set your speed again. *After:* Dolly keeps the speed you chose through playback, seeks and recordings, and only returns the game to 1x when the session closes.

- **Zooming a held shot no longer changes the previous camera.** *Before:* keeping the camera still for a holding shot, moving the replay on and zooming in for the next camera also changed the previous camera's zoom. *After:* that zoom only goes into the next camera you capture; zooming while you're on a saved camera's moment still edits that camera.

- **Clearer in-game status messages.** *Before:* some in-game status lines ended in a question mark, such as "Letting replay effects settle before the shot?". *After:* they end with a period or three dots as intended.
