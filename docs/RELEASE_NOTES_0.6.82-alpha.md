### Deadlock Dolly v0.6.82-alpha

**0.6.82-alpha**

- **Redesigned interface.** *Before:* The desktop and in-game controls used the older layouts. *After:* Kara's new desktop interface and in-game rail bring replay, camera, Look, curves and export controls together, while keeping existing shots and camera features.

- **Camera path controls.** *Before:* Path actions were spread across the Camera drawer and Replay bar, and seeking a final camera between replay packets could fail. *After:* Seek camera, Replace selected and Save Path sit together in Campaths, and paused endpoint seeking holds the saved camera and effects at the next recorded packet.

- **Bone Picker.** *Before:* Scene joint circles could disappear and attached preview had a duplicate button. *After:* Click blue joint circles with contrasting rings, and switch between overview and attached preview using the tabs at the top.

- **Startup messages.** *Before:* A game exit could appear as a console timeout, or an older dump could be mistaken for the current failure. *After:* Dolly reports startup exits more clearly and offers overlay troubleshooting without automatically relaunching the game.
